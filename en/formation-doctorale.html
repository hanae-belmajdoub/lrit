Doctoral training

A supervised research pathway in LRIT’s scientific fields.

Pursue a PhD at LRIT

Doctoral training is based on a research project supervised within the laboratory. It combines a review of the scientific literature, formulation of a research problem, selection of methods, experimentation and dissemination of results.

Research fields

  • Artificial intelligence and data science
  • Signal processing, image and vision
  • Networks, IoT and cybersecurity
  • Telecommunications and antennas
  • Embedded systems and architectures
  • Intelligent systems and applications
  • Software engineering
Explore research areas ↗

The research pathway

Formulate the research problem

Analyse existing work, refine the research questions and define objectives with the supervisor.

Develop and evaluate methods

Implement the selected approaches, organise experiments and analyse the results.

Present the results

Prepare conference contributions, publications and the thesis manuscript, with scientific oversight of the work carried out.

Prepare your application

Consult research topics and admissions calls, identify supervisors whose work relates to your project and prepare the documents requested in the official announcement. Enrolment, re-enrolment and progress monitoring are governed by the doctoral studies centre’s institutional information.
