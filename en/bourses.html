SUPPORTING RESEARCH

Scholarships and doctoral support

Funding and mobility schemes for doctoral students.

The information below records schemes and awards documented at the laboratory. Current calls, criteria, amounts and deadlines should be checked in the institutional announcements for the relevant application cycle.
ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE

Excellence scholarships

LRIT doctoral students have received excellence scholarships to support their work and research training. An Eiffel scholarship is also documented for an internationally jointly supervised doctorate.

DOCTORAL TRAINING

Moniteur PASS

Moniteur PASS is among the national support schemes that have benefited the laboratory’s doctoral students. Eight scholarships were awarded to LRIT in the 2023 round.

DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY & INNOVATION

Moniteur Numérique

This scheme is listed among the forms of support for doctoral work related to digital transformation, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

The documented awards combine the PASS and Moniteur Numérique schemes: 14 scholarships in 2024 and 12 in 2025.

MOBILITY & COOPERATION

Partner support

Master’s and DESA internships have been funded by partner laboratories. Scientific cooperation also enables international joint supervision and cotutelle arrangements.
