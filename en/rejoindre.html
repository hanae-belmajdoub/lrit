Why join LRIT?

Prepare a thesis, develop a scientific project and contribute to research within the laboratory.

Develop your research project at LRIT

Would you like to pursue a PhD, investigate a scientific question or propose a collaboration? LRIT brings together faculty and doctoral researchers in computer science, information processing and telecommunications. Joining the laboratory allows you to develop a project in these fields in contact with a research community.

01

Complementary research fields

Artificial intelligence, data, signals, images, networks, cybersecurity, antennas and software engineering. This diversity allows a problem to be approached using several methods and connects fundamental research with applications.

02

Scientific supervision

A doctoral project is developed with a supervisor around a research problem, methodology and results to analyse. The directory helps you identify faculty and explore their specialisms and work.

03

A community for exchanging ideas

Seminars, research days and conferences provide opportunities to present results, discuss ideas and develop scientific communication skills.

04

Collaborations and applications

The laboratory’s projects connect research with issues in health, the environment, mobility and digital systems. Academic and socioeconomic collaborations contribute to this work, including through joint supervision and cotutelle arrangements.

How can you propose a project?

  1. Identify a field and a contact. Consult research areas, publications and member profiles to position your project.
  2. Present your approach. Prepare a brief description of your research question, objectives, skills and intended timeframe.
  3. Choose the appropriate pathway. For a PhD, consult doctoral training information and UM5 institutional announcements. For an internship or collaboration, contact a member whose research matches your topic.
