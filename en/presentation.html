About and history

At the Faculty of Sciences of Rabat, LRIT conducts research at the intersection of computer science, information processing and telecommunications.

Originating in the Signals, Communications and Multimedia group founded in 1993, the laboratory combines scientific research, doctoral training and cooperation. Its work ranges from digital content to networks and embedded systems.
