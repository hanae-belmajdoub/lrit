01 / THE PROGRAMME

A solid foundation.
Open horizons.

Master IT combines fundamental teaching in information and communication science and technology with specialisation and an introduction to research.

The programme develops broad skills in computer science and telecommunications before allowing students to specialise in a field related to research areas and applications of interest.

01Consolidate scientific and technical foundations.
02Specialise in computer science, AI or telecommunications.
03Develop a methodological approach to research.

05 / ADMISSIONS

The Master’s is offered with standard and adjusted schedules. Arrangements for each format should be checked with the programme team.

Who is the programme for?

Holders of a fundamental studies bachelor’s degree in SMI, SMP, networks and telecommunications, computer science, or a qualification considered equivalent, subject to the requirements of the admissions cycle.

07 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few pointers
before you begin.

What are the two options?

Computer Science and Applied AI, and Networks and Telecommunications. The specialist modules are presented in the semester 3 curriculum.

Is an adjusted schedule available?

The brochure describes both standard and adjusted schedules. Contact the programme coordinator for the timetable and arrangements for a specific admissions cycle.

How do I apply?

Consult the official call from the Faculty of Sciences of Rabat. It specifies the procedure, required documents and dates. This website does not collect applications.

What does the final semester involve?

Semester 4 is devoted to the final-year project. Its arrangements and supervision are specified by the programme team.

Where can I find timetables and results?

Contact the programme coordinator and consult the programme’s institutional channels. These documents will be added here when they are available for distribution.
